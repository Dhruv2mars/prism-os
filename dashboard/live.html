Why the previous run stalled

22 verdicts, every one a fail. Ours scored 47-59/90 against visionOS at 81-83/90.

  1. The loop never looped: 10 of 16 pieces sat at FAIL with no builder ever sent back.
  2. Focus discipline named the biggest gap on 9 pieces across 11 verdicts, fixed on none.
  3. Engineering tokens ('tabular', 'prism-ease · 260ms') shipped in user-visible copy on 5 pieces.
  4. Web Apps was a picture of a platform: zero iframe, zero sandbox, zero manifest, zero postMessage.
  5. The Meta developer platform was in scope and was silently dropped.
  6. 328MB of screenshots across 411 PNGs; nothing committed for 8 hours of work.

What is different this time

  1. tools/probe.mjs is now an enforcing GATE (exit 1 on violation), not a reporter. It fails the build on dev chrome inside the stage, engineering tokens in rendered text, stage overflow, unhonored reduced motion, unnamed focusables, and any console or page error.
  2. os/shared/lab.js moves every developer control OUT of the 600x600 stage into a fixed rail, and drives all five edge states from the URL so critics can screenshot them.
  3. os/shared/lab.js also paints a world layer behind the glass, so material honesty is judgeable instead of assumed.
  4. The critic's disqualification rule lives in .claude/agents/prism-critic.md frontmatter, so it survives a spawn prompt that forgets to repeat it.
  5. No piece is ever left at FAIL without a live builder on it.

Coordinate bug inside frozen os/shared/core.js (targetFromPoint)PARTIALLY WORKED AROUND

core.js converts the pointer to stage-local coordinates by dividing by state.scale, then passes those to document.elementFromPoint(), which expects viewport coordinates. Under any stage transform the hit test resolves to the wrong element or to none, so .gaze-focus never lands where the wearer is looking.

Workaround. Section 10 freezes core.js, so the correction lives in hardenGaze() in os/shared/lab.js: a viewport-space hit test registered after core's own listener, which therefore has the last word on .gaze-focus. Every piece already calls hardenGaze, so the fix is systemic rather than per-piece. Verified: .gaze-focus now settles on the element under the gaze at 310% text scale.

Residual. The ACTIVATION path is still wrong. core.js pointerdown calls focused.click() against its own mis-resolved element, so a click can fire on an element the wearer was not looking at. The lab-side listener corrects the highlight, not the dispatch. Fixing it needs either an edit to frozen core.js or a capture-phase interceptor that swallows core's pointerdown, and neither is in scope without permission to touch the frozen file. Recorded here rather than dropped.
